TumourLens

Measure spheroids from microscope images and turn a plate map into dose-response curves. Everything runs in your browser; images are never uploaded.

1Add images

2Check the outlines

Untick any image whose outline is wrong; it's left out of the results. Lower the sensitivity if outlines are too small, raise it if they're too big.

3Plate map

One row per image: file,condition,value. The value is a dose or a time, and files can be matched by part of their name (for example a well ID).

How accurate is the outlining?

Tested against expert-drawn outlines on images the method was never tuned on. Dice measures overlap: 1.0 is a perfect match, 0.8 or more is a good outline.

It works best when the spheroid is more textured than its background. Smooth spheroids on a grainy background can be missed, so always check the outlines in step 2.
